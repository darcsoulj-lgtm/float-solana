# Markets information hierarchy

The market overview uses short metric labels and values first. Coverage caveats that affect interpretation remain visible (partial and dated tracked value, partial pool coverage, and mixed supply bases); definitions and source rules stay in the adjacent info controls and existing Coverage & methodology disclosure. Source-delay names remain one tap away under a visible warning.

History appears as a chart only after seven observed days in the selected range. Before then, show the observed-day count and a collection state, without a lone bar that could imply a trend. Missing days remain gaps; no values are estimated. The history disclosure explains the collection rule.

This is a presentation change. The market data API, source freshness, aggregation, issuer mapping, table filters, and wallet/community access rules remain owned by their existing modules. Check phone and desktop layouts in both themes, the warning disclosure, keyboard access, and chart transition when sufficient observations accumulate.
