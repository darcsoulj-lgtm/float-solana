# Small real-user validation kit

Proposed next experiment; no participants or results have been invented and no invitations have been sent.

Recruit 5–10 people who already hold supported Solana tokenized stocks. Do not ask anyone to buy an asset for testing. Avoid incentives that would distort whether they actually want the community.

## Invitation draft

I'm testing Float, a community and market dashboard for Solana tokenized-stock holders. If you already hold a supported token, would you try the wallet sign-in, read a discussion and tell me what is confusing or missing? Signing is a message only, with no transaction or transfer. You can also explore Markets without a wallet. The test takes about 10 minutes: https://joinfloat.xyz

## Tasks and questions

- Before explaining Float, ask: What do you think this product is for?
- Ask them to find an asset in public Markets and explain one displayed number.
- If eligible, ask them to join, open a channel and find how to create or reply to a post.
- Ask: Where do you currently talk about these holdings? What would make you return here?
- Ask: What almost made you stop? Which information would you expect to remain private?
- Follow up after seven days: Did you return voluntarily, and why?

Proposed first gate: at least 4 of 5 eligible testers complete sign-in without founder assistance; at least 3 make a meaningful contribution; at least 2 return voluntarily within a week. These are small-sample learning thresholds, not evidence of product-market fit. If sign-in fails, repair it before recruiting more. If people can use Float but do not return, investigate the content/value problem before adding features.

Business hypothesis: start with a free community; test willingness to pay for premium research workflows only after repeat usage. No revenue, partnership or user count should be entered into the application without records.
