# Float logo

Production mark: `public/brand/float-mark.svg`. Shared wordmark/mark component: `components/float-logo.tsx`. The mark is the vector #03 refined direction and works in both theme colors.

## Generation prompt
Create a finished production logo SYMBOL ONLY for Float, a refined community for holders of tokenized equities. Meaning: public float and freely exchanging perspectives. Design one distinctive, expertly balanced minimal abstract lowercase f, formed from TWO detached bold soft geometric ribbon shapes, an upper horizontal floating capsule with a gently upward curled left end, and beneath it a short forward-extending crossbar flowing into a descending vertical stem with a softly curved lower terminal. It should feel buoyant and precise, with generous negative space, sturdy silhouette readable at 24 pixels, subtly asymmetric, no tiny detail. Flat single solid coral red #E84350, with truly transparent alpha background. No white square, no background, no gradient, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no lettering or wordmark, no border, no enclosing tile, no mockup, no chart arrows, no coins, no generic chat bubble. Square asset, centered symbol occupying 85 percent of canvas height and width with balanced modest transparent margins. High quality vector-like edges. Deliver a single final logo symbol, not multiple concepts.
